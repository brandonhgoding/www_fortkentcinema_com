Open Mic · Every Monday

Take center stage

The stage in the Century auditorium is yours. Sing karaoke, play songs you wrote, try a stand-up set, or just come and cheer. Put your name on the sign-up sheet at the door. It’s free and all ages. Doors open at 6:00 PM and the first act goes up at 6:30 PM.
