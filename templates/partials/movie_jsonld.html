{% comment %} schema.org Movie for /movies/, with a ScreeningEvent per UPCOMING showtime. Only upcoming showtimes become ScreeningEvents — advertising a screening that has already happened is wrong, and Google will ignore or penalise it. Archive films emit a Movie with no events, which is correct. Optional fields (contentRating, sameAs, trailer) are omitted entirely rather than emitted empty; a blank value is worse than an absent one. {% endcomment %}
