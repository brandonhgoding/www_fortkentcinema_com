WHAT IT IS

The movie is the straight man. You’re the comedy.

Once a month we put a gloriously bad old movie on the big screen, bring the house lights halfway up, and let the room tear it apart. Flying saucers on strings. Monsters in gorilla suits. Dialogue nobody should ever have said out loud, said out loud anyway. You are invited — encouraged, really — to talk back to all of it.

If you’ve seen Mystery Science Theater 3000, you already know how this works. If you haven’t: a roomful of people watching a terrible film together turns out to be funnier than the film ever was, and that only happens in a room. It is the one kind of movie night you cannot get at home on the couch.

{% comment %} House rules {% endcomment %}

HOW IT WORKS

  • Shout it out. Jokes, groans, running gags, sound effects. The movie can take it; it has had decades of practice.
  • Heckle the movie, not each other. The one rule that matters. Everyone in the room is on the same team, and the film is the only thing in here without feelings.
  • Keep it PG. This night is {{ heckle_night.audience|lower }}, and there will be kids in the room. Aim the joke somewhere a ten-year-old can follow it.
  • Short and sharp beats long. Get the laugh and let the next person have a turn. A bit that outlasts the scene stops being a bit.
  • Don’t drown out the film. If the people two rows over can’t hear the next line, nobody gets to joke about it.
  • Sit up front. Fill the front half before the back. A room spread thin doesn’t laugh, and this one needs to.

WHY THESE MOVIES

Every Heckle Night film is in the public domain — made decades ago, owned by nobody now, and ready for a second life as a comedy. That is not a compromise, it is the whole premise. A film has to be a little bit terrible before a room full of people can improve it, and the ones that fell out of copyright are terrible in the most generous possible way: earnest, cheap, and completely convinced they are working.

It also means the night costs us nothing to book, which is how a seat here is ${{ heckle_night.price }} instead of what a first-run ticket has to be.
