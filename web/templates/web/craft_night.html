WHAT IT IS

A movie night for people who can’t sit still with empty hands.

Once a month we run a screening with the house lights dimmed rather than killed — low enough for the picture to look right, bright enough to find your place in a row of stitches. Bring whatever you’re working on. Knitting and crochet, cross stitch, embroidery, hand quilting, beadwork, a sketchbook, a coloring book, a journal. Sit with it for two hours and let someone else do the entertaining.

We run this one with {{ craft_night.partner }}, a few streets over on Market Street. Their members pay our member rate. Everyone else is welcome at regular evening admission — you don’t need to belong to anything to come, and you don’t need to bring a project. Plenty of people just come for the movie.

{% comment %} House rules {% endcomment %}

HOW IT WORKS

  • The lights stay on, dimmed. Not a dark house. You’ll be able to see your work without a headlamp.
  • Bring your own project. We don’t supply materials and we don’t teach. This is a room to work in, not a class.
  • Keep it to your seat. If it fits on your lap and in the seat beside you, it’s fine. Sewing machines, easels and anything that needs a table or an outlet are not going to work in here.
  • Nothing wet, nothing loose. No paint, no glue, no glitter — the same rule our rentals have, for the same reason. Please keep blades away from the upholstery.
  • Quiet counts. Talk between friends is expected. Anything that clatters is not.
  • Concessions are open as usual. Popcorn is, as ever, a one-handed food.
