WHAT IT IS

One stage, one room, and whoever turns up.

Every {{ open_mic.day_label }} night we gather in {{ open_mic.room }} for open mic. Sing karaoke. Play the song you wrote last week. Try out some stand-up. Read a poem, do a magic trick, or do something we haven’t thought of. If you can do it on a stage, it counts.

First-timers and regulars get the same welcome, and the room is on your side. Not performing? Come anyway. The audience is half of an open mic.

  3. {{ open_mic.end_label }} We wrap up, or earlier once everyone on the sheet has had a turn.
{% comment %} House rules {% endcomment %}

HOW IT WORKS

  • Sign up at the door. First come, first served. There’s nothing to book ahead.
  • Keep it PG. Open mic is {{ open_mic.audience|lower }}, and there may be kids in the room and on the stage. That goes for song lyrics too.
  • Cheer for everyone. Everyone who gets up is taking a chance. Clap for the nervous ones loudest.
  • Leave time for the next act. The sheet has to fit before 9:00, so keep your set short and let the next person have their turn.
